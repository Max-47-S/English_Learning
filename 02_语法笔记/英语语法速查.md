# 英语语法速查

[返回语法笔记](README.md)

[句子成分](#句子成分) · [句子结构](#句子结构) · [特殊句式](#特殊句式) · [常见英文缩写](#常见英文缩写) · [时态标志词](#时态标志词) · [拆句示范与自测](#拆句示范与自测)

遇到长句时，先找主句的谓语动词组，再找它的主语以及后面的宾语、表语或补足语。最后看其他部分是修饰名词，还是补充动作的时间、地点、原因或条件。

## 句子成分

词性说的是一个词属于哪一类，句子成分说的是它在这句话里做什么。`book` 在 *The book fell* 中属于主语，在 *I bought a book* 中属于宾语，两处都还是名词。

| 记号 | 成分与英文名 | 在句子里做什么 | 例子 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| S | 主语 · subject | 句子主要谈论的人、事物或情况 | **The lights** went out. 灯灭了。 |
| V | 谓语动词组 · verb phrase | 说主语做什么或处于什么状态。助动词、情态动词和主要动词可以一起看 | The lights **have gone out**. 灯已经灭了。 |
| O | 宾语 · object | 动词所涉及的人、事物或内容 | I checked **the schedule**. 我查了日程。 |
| IO / DO | 间接宾语 · indirect object / 直接宾语 · direct object | 双宾语中常分别表示接受者和所给的东西 | She sent **me**（IO）**a link**（DO）. 她给我发了个链接。 |
| SC | 表语／主语补足语 · subject complement | 在系动词后说明主语的身份或状态 | The link looks **safe**. 这个链接看起来很安全。 |
| OC | 宾语补足语 · object complement | 说明宾语的身份、状态或变化结果 | Keep the door **open**. 让门开着。 |
| A | 状语 · adverbial | 交代动作的时间、地点、方式、频率、原因等 | We met **after class**. 我们下课后见了面。 |
| — | 定语／名词修饰语 · noun modifier | 说明是哪个人或事物，或是什么样的 | I opened the file **on my desktop**. 我打开了桌面上的文件。 |
| — | 同位语 · appositive | 给旁边的名词或名词短语换一种说法 | Maya, **my study partner**, called. 我的学习搭档玛雅打来电话。 |

- **宾语和表语**。`I met a doctor.` 中 `a doctor` 是我遇到的人。`I became a doctor.` 中 `a doctor` 说明我的新身份。
- **双宾语和宾补**。`They gave Maya a key.` 中 `Maya` 收到了 `a key`。`They made Maya team leader.` 中 `team leader` 说明 `Maya` 成了什么。
- **定语和状语**。`The book on the desk is mine.` 中 `on the desk` 修饰 `book`。`I studied at the desk.` 中 `at the desk` 交代学习地点。判断时看它依附哪个词。 [Cambridge · 补足语](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/complements) · [Cambridge · 附加语](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/adjuncts)

## 句子结构

### 七种常见基本骨架

这里用 `C` 表示表语或宾语补足语。`SVA`、`SVOA` 中的 `A` 指该用法所需的地点等成分。有的语法书也把这类必需成分叫补足语。 [Cambridge · 补足语](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/complements)

| 骨架 | 常用叫法 | 例句与拆法 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| SV | 主谓 | The baby（S）slept（V）. 宝宝睡了。 |
| SVC | 主系表 | The room（S）became（V）quiet（C）. 房间安静了下来。 |
| SVO | 主谓宾 | I（S）found（V）the key（O）. 我找到了钥匙。 |
| SVOO | 主谓双宾 | She（S）sent（V）me（IO）a link（DO）. 她给我发了个链接。 |
| SVOC | 主谓宾宾补 | The news（S）made（V）me（O）happy（C）. 这个消息让我很开心。 |
| SVA | 主谓加必要状语 | The keys（S）are（V）on the desk（A）. 钥匙在桌上。 |
| SVOA | 主谓宾加必要状语 | She（S）put（V）the keys（O）on the desk（A）. 她把钥匙放在了桌上。 |

`I found the key yesterday.` 仍可用 `SVO` 记骨架，`yesterday` 额外交代时间。`She put the keys on the desk.` 中，`on the desk` 补足了 `put` 所要求的落点。

### 从短句到长句

| 类型 | 组成 | 例句 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 简单句 · simple sentence | 一个独立分句。主语或谓语可以并列 | I checked the schedule and booked a seat. 我查了日程，订了一个座位。 |
| 并列句 · compound sentence | 两个或以上独立分句 | I called, but nobody answered. 我打了电话，但没人接。 |
| 主从复合句 · complex sentence | 一个主句加至少一个从句 | I called because I needed help. 我打电话是因为需要帮忙。 |
| 并列复合句 · compound-complex sentence | 至少两个独立分句加至少一个从句 | I called because I needed help, but nobody answered. 我因为需要帮忙打了电话，但没人接。 |

从句也能占据句子里的一个位置。 [Cambridge · 分句与句子](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/sentences-and-clauses)

| 从句的工作 | 例句 | 怎么判断 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 作主语 | **What she said** surprised me. 她说的话让我吃惊。 | 整个粗体部分是让人吃惊的内容。 |
| 作宾语 | I think **(that) the file is ready**. 我觉得文件已经准备好了。 | 整个粗体部分回答“我认为怎样”。 |
| 作表语 | The problem is **that we have no time**. 问题是我们没时间。 | 粗体部分说明问题是什么。 |
| 补充名词的内容 | The news **that the class was cancelled** surprised me. 停课的消息让我吃惊。 | 粗体部分说出 `news` 的具体内容，传统语法常称同位语从句。 |
| 作定语 | The file **that you sent** opened normally. 你发的文件能正常打开。 | 粗体部分限定是哪一份文件。 |
| 作状语 | I'll call **when I arrive**. 我到了就打电话。 | 粗体部分说明什么时候打电话。 |

### `that` 能不能省

| 类型 | 例句 | 句中作用与省略 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 内容从句的连接词 | I think **(that)** she knows the answer. | `she knows the answer` 的主语和宾语齐全。`that` 负责引出内容，`think`、`say`、`hope` 等后面常可省。 |
| 关系词作宾语 | This is the file **(that)** you sent. | 补回原意是 `you sent the file`。限定性定语从句里的宾语关系词常可省。 |
| 关系词作主语 | This is the file **that** contains our notes. | 补回原意是 `the file contains our notes`。这里保留关系词。 |
| 内容从句位于句首作主语 | **That** she left early surprised me. | 这个位置保留句首的 `that`，整个从句作主语。 |
| 指示代词或限定词 | **That** is mine. / I want **that** book. | `that` 指“那个”，前一句作主语，后一句限定 `book`。 |

带逗号的补充性定语从句用 `who`、`which` 等关系词，并保留它们。`My laptop, which I bought last year, still works well.` 中，`which I bought last year` 补充电脑的购买时间。 [Cambridge · that 从句](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/that-clauses) · [Cambridge · 关系代词](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/relative)

### 谓语与非谓语一起找

| 形式 | 例句 | 识别方法 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 带时态的谓语动词组 | She **has been studying**. | 整组作谓语，`has` 承担现在时和人称变化，`studying` 是其中的主要动词形式。 |
| `to` 不定式 | She **wants** *to leave*. | `wants` 是主句的谓语动词，`to leave` 是 `wants` 后接的非谓语结构。 |
| `-ing` 形式 | *Reading aloud* **helps**. | `Reading aloud` 整体作主语，`helps` 是主句的谓语动词。 |
| 过去分词 | The files *sent yesterday* **are** on my desk. | `sent yesterday` 修饰 `files`，可展开成 `that were sent yesterday`。 |

读到 `may be obvious`、`doesn't appear` 时，把情态动词或助动词和后面的动词连起来看。再检查有没有 `to leave`、`sent yesterday` 这样的非谓语结构。

## 特殊句式

| 句式 | 可套用的骨架 | 例句与用法 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 一般疑问句 | `be / 助动词 / 情态动词 + S + …?` | `Has she finished?` 她做完了吗？一般现在或过去的实义动词句常用 `do / does / did`，如 `Did she finish?` |
| 特殊疑问句 | `疑问词 + 助动词 + S + …?` | `Who did you call?` 你给谁打了电话？`who` 作宾语。 |
| 主语疑问句 | `疑问词作 S + V + …?` | `Who called you?` 谁给你打了电话？`who` 作主语，普通提问直接接 `called`。 |
| 间接问句 | `Do you know + 疑问词 / whether / if + 陈述语序?` | `Do you know where the station is?` 你知道车站在哪儿吗？内层用 `the station is` 的语序。 |
| 反意疑问句 | `陈述句 + 简短问尾` | `You've saved it, haven't you?` 你已经保存了，对吧？用前句的助动词，常见搭配是肯定陈述接否定问尾。 |
| 祈使句 | `V1 + …` 或 `Don't + V1 + …` | `Save the file first.` 先保存文件。`Don't close the window.` 别关窗口。 |
| 感叹句 | `What + 名词短语 + (S + V)!` 或 `How + adj./adv. + (S + V)!` | `What a useful tip!` 这个建议真有用！`How quickly she learns!` 她学得真快！ |
| 存在句 | `There + be + 名词短语` | `There are two copies on the desk.` 桌上有两份。书面表达中可按后面名词短语的单复数选 `is / are`。 |
| 形式主语 | `It + be + adj. + to V / that 从句` | `It's easy to miss the last word.` 最后一个词很容易漏听。具体内容由 `to miss the last word` 交代。 |
| 形式宾语 | `S + find / make / think + it + C + to V / that 从句` | `I find it hard to hear the ending.` 我觉得听清词尾很难。`it` 先占宾语位置，后面说清难在哪里。 |
| 强调句 · it-cleft | `It is / was + 焦点 + that / who + 其余内容` | `It was Maya who sent the link.` 发链接的是玛雅。普通语序是 `Maya sent the link.` |
| 否定表达前置的倒装 | `Never / Not until … + 助动词 + S + …` | `Never have I heard that sound before.` 我以前从未听过那个声音。倒装发生在主句的助动词和主语之间。 |
| 祈使式条件表达 | `V1 + …, and / or + 陈述句` | `Save a copy, or you may lose your work.` 存一份副本，否则可能丢掉成果。 |
| 被动语态 | `be + V3` | `The files were sent yesterday.` 文件昨天发出去了。`were` 表过去时，`sent` 是过去分词。 |
| 结果句式 | `so + adj./adv. + that …` / `such + 名词短语 + that …` | `It was so quiet that I could hear the clock.` 安静得我能听见钟声。`It was such a clear explanation that I took notes.` 解释很清楚，我还记了笔记。 |
| 递进比较 | `The + 比较级 …, the + 比较级 …` | `The more you listen, the easier it gets.` 你听得越多，就越容易。 |
| 程度与不定式 | `too + adj./adv. + to V` / `adj./adv. + enough + to V` | `The audio is too fast to follow.` 音频快得跟不上。`It's slow enough to follow.` 速度慢到可以跟上。 |

强调句可以用还原语序的办法确认焦点。`It was yesterday that she called.` 还原为 `She called yesterday.`。在 `It was clear that she needed help.` 里，`that` 从句交代“什么很明显”，`it` 是形式主语。 [Cambridge · 强调句](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/uk/grammar/british-grammar/cleft) · [Cambridge · 疑问句与祈使句](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/statements-questions)

### 三种常用条件句

| 想表达什么 | 常用结构 | 例句 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 将来可能发生 | `if + 一般现在时，主句常用 will / can + V1` | If it rains tomorrow, we'll stay in. 明天下雨的话，我们就待在家里。 |
| 现在或将来的假设 | `if + 过去式，主句常用 would / could + V1` | If I had more time, I'd read the whole book. 我要是时间更多，就会把整本书读完。 |
| 回想过去没有发生的情况 | `if + had + V3，主句常用 would / could have + V3` | If I'd checked the time, I'd have caught the train. 当时我要是看了时间，就赶上火车了。 |

第二行用过去式表达对现在或将来的假设。第三行同一个句子中的两个 `I'd` 分别是 `I had` 和 `I would`。

## 常见英文缩写

### 听读里最常见的缩约

| 缩约 | 展开 | 例句 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `I'm / you're / we're / they're` | `I am / you are / we are / they are` | `We're ready.` 我们准备好了。 |
| `he's / she's / it's / that's` | `he / she / it / that + is 或 has` | `She's ready.` → `She is ready.` 她准备好了。`She's left.` → `She has left.` 她已经走了。 |
| `I've / you've / we've / they've` | `I / you / we / they + have` | `I've seen it.` 我看过了。 |
| `I'll / you'll / we'll` | `I / you / we + will` | `I'll call later.` 我晚点打电话。 |
| `I'd / you'd / she'd` | `I / you / she + had 或 would` | `I'd left.` → `I had left.` 我当时已经走了。`I'd leave.` → `I would leave.` 我会走。 |
| `isn't / aren't / wasn't / weren't` | `is / are / was / were + not` | `They weren't ready.` 他们当时还没准备好。 |
| `don't / doesn't / didn't` | `do / does / did + not` | `I didn't hear it.` 我没听到。`didn't` 后用 `hear` 的原形。 |
| `haven't / hasn't / hadn't` | `have / has / had + not` | `I haven't finished yet.` 我还没做完。 |
| `can't / couldn't / won't / wouldn't` | `cannot / could not / will not / would not` | `I couldn't hear you.` 我刚才听不清你说话。 |
| `shouldn't / mustn't / mightn't` | `should / must / might + not` | `You shouldn't skip the intro.` 你不该跳过开头。 |
| `could've / should've / would've` | `could / should / would + have` | `You should've called.` 你当时应该打个电话。书面展开时用 `have`。 |
| `let's` | `let us`，常用于提议 | `Let's try again.` 我们再试一次吧。 |

- **辨认 `'d`**。`You'd be reminded` 中，`be` 是原形，`'d` 展开为 `would`。`You'd been told` 中，`been` 是过去分词，`'d` 展开为 `had`。固定表达 `you'd better leave` 展开为 `you had better leave`。
- **辨认 `'s`**。`She's checking` 是 `She is checking`。`She's checked the file` 是 `She has checked the file`。`The file's been checked` 是 `The file has been checked`。`Maya's file` 中的 `'s` 表示所属。 [Cambridge · 缩约](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/contractions)
- **`ain't`** 在歌曲、方言和非正式口语中可以对应 `am not / is not / are not / has not / have not`。`He ain't ready` 对应 `He isn't ready`，`She ain't seen it yet` 对应 `She hasn't seen it yet`。 [Cambridge · ain't](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/us/dictionary/english/ain-t)
- **`gonna / wanna / gotta`** 常见于口语写法。`I'm gonna go` 对应 `I'm going to go`，`I wanna go` 对应 `I want to go`，`I've gotta go` 对应 `I've got to go`。写正式文本时可使用这些展开形式。

### 词典和语法书里的记号

| 记号 | 全称与意思 |
| --- | --- |
| `POS` | part of speech，词性 |
| `n. / v. / adj. / adv.` | noun / verb / adjective / adverb，名词／动词／形容词／副词 |
| `pron. / prep. / conj. / det.` | pronoun / preposition / conjunction / determiner，代词／介词／连词／限定词 |
| `vt. / vi.` 或 `[T] / [I]` | transitive / intransitive，及物／不及物 |
| `[C] / [U]` | countable / uncountable，可数／不可数。句型里的 `C` 则是 complement。 |
| `sb / sth` | somebody / something，词典中的人或事物占位符。如 `remind sb of sth` 可以换成 `remind me of home`。 |
| `V1 / V2 / V3 / V-ing` | 动词原形／过去式／过去分词／`-ing` 形式。如 `go / went / gone / going`。 |

需要更细的词性标签可查 [英语词性英文全称与缩写](英语词性英文全称与缩写.md)。

### 邮件、聊天和日常书面缩写

| 缩写 | 展开 | 用法 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `e.g.` | *exempli gratia*（for example） | 举例，如 `Bring a snack, e.g., an apple.` |
| `i.e.` | *id est*（that is） | 换句话说明，如 `one week, i.e., seven days`。 |
| `etc.` | *et cetera*（and so on） | “等等”，如 `pens, paper, etc.` |
| `a.m. / p.m.` | *ante meridiem* / *post meridiem* | 12 小时制中的上午／下午或晚间。如 `9 a.m.` 和 `9 p.m.`。中午和午夜可直接写 `noon` 和 `midnight`。 |
| `FYI` | for your information | 供你参考，如 `FYI, the meeting has moved to Friday.` |
| `ASAP` | as soon as possible | 尽快，如 `Please reply ASAP.` |
| `ETA` | estimated time of arrival | 预计到达时间，如 `What's your ETA?` |
| `TBD` | to be determined | 待定，如 `Location: TBD.` |
| `RSVP` | *répondez s'il vous plaît*（please respond） | 邀请函中请收件人回复，如 `Please RSVP by Friday.` |
| `BTW / IDK / IMO` | by the way / I don't know / in my opinion | 聊天中表示“顺便说一下／我不知道／在我看来”。 |

## 时态标志词

时间词是线索。先确认说话人以现在、过去还是将来为参照点，再看是在说习惯、某刻进行的动作、截至某时的结果，还是持续过程。最后核对谓语的实际形式。

| 时态 | 常见谓语 | 常见时间线索 | 例句 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 一般现在时 | `V1 / V-s`，或 `am/is/are` | `usually`、`often`、`every day`、`on Mondays` | I usually study after dinner. 我通常晚饭后学习。 |
| 现在进行时 | `am/is/are + V-ing` | `right now`、`at the moment`、`currently`、`this week` | I'm studying right now. 我现在正在学习。 |
| 现在完成时 | `have/has + V3` | `ever`、`never`、`already`、`yet`、`so far`、`since + 起点` | I've finished three lessons so far. 到目前我已学完三课。 |
| 现在完成进行时 | `have/has been + V-ing` | `since + 起点`、`for + 时长`、`all morning`、`lately` | I've been studying since eight. 我从八点一直学到现在。 |
| 一般过去时 | `V2`，或 `was/were` | `yesterday`、`last week`、`two days ago`、`in 2020` | I finished it yesterday. 我昨天做完的。 |
| 过去进行时 | `was/were + V-ing` | `at eight last night`、`at that moment`、`when …`、`while …` | I was studying when she called. 她打电话时我正在学习。 |
| 过去完成时 | `had + V3` | `by then`、`by the time + 过去事件`、`before + 过去事件` | By the time she called, I'd finished. 她打电话时我已经做完了。 |
| 过去完成进行时 | `had been + V-ing` | `for + 时长 + before / when + 过去事件` | I'd been studying for two hours when she called. 她打电话时，我已经学了两小时。 |
| 一般将来表达 | `will + V1` 或 `be going to + V1` | `tomorrow`、`next week`、`soon`、`later` | I'll call tomorrow. 我明天打电话。 |
| 将来进行时 | `will be + V-ing` | `at eight tomorrow`、`this time tomorrow` | I'll be studying at eight tomorrow. 明天八点我会在学习。 |
| 将来完成时 | `will have + V3` | `by Friday`、`by the end of next month` | I'll have finished the report by Friday. 到周五我就会写完报告。 |
| 将来完成进行时 | `will have been + V-ing` | `by + 将来时间`，同时交代 `for + 时长` | By noon tomorrow, I'll have been studying for four hours. 到明天中午我就连续学了四个小时。 |

**过去将来**从过去的某个时点往后看。常见线索有 `the next day`、`later that week`、`by the following Friday`，还要结合 `said`、`thought`、`expected` 等建立的过去视角。 [Cambridge · 将来表达](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/future)

| 四种形式 | 例句 |
| --- | --- |
| `would + V1` | She said she'd call the next day. 她说第二天会打电话。 |
| `would be + V-ing` | She said she'd be working at nine the next morning. 她说第二天早上九点她会在工作。 |
| `would have + V3` | She said she'd have finished by the following Friday. 她说到接下来的周五就会做完。 |
| `would have been + V-ing` | She said that by noon she'd have been working for four hours. 她说到中午就已经连续工作四小时了。 |

### 容易混的时间线索

| 线索 | 放进句子里比较 |
| --- | --- |
| `for` 表时长，`since` 表起点 | `I worked there for two years.` 表示过去做了两年。`I've worked here for two years.` 可表示在这里工作至今两年。`I've worked here since 2024.` 给出起点。 [Cambridge · for 与 since](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/for-or-since) |
| 已结束的过去时间与截至现在 | `I saw her yesterday.` 把见面放在昨天。`I've seen her twice this week.` 把本周视为延续到现在的时段。 [Cambridge · 一般过去时和现在完成时](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/us/grammar/british-grammar/past-simple-or-present-perfect-2) |
| `by`、`until` 和 `at` | `Finish it by Friday.` 最迟周五完成。`Work on it until Friday.` 持续做到周五。`Meet at six.` 六点见。`by` 本身就能表示期限，如 `I'll finish it by Friday.` |
| `tomorrow` 与将来安排 | `The train leaves at nine tomorrow.` 用一般现在时说时刻表。`I'm meeting her tomorrow.` 用现在进行时说已约好的安排。 [Cambridge · 一般现在时表示将来](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/simple-future) |
| `when / if` 与未来时间、条件 | `I'll call when I arrive.` 时间从句常用现在形式表示将来。`Do you know when she'll arrive?` 中 `when` 引出间接问句，可用 `will`。 [Cambridge · when 的用法](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/when) |
| `before / after` 与先后 | `After I finished, I went home.` 连词已说明顺序。`When I got home, she'd already left.` 用过去完成时突出她离开发生在我到家之前。 |

完整的 16 种形式可和 [时态](时态.md) 对照。

## 拆句示范与自测

> This password doesn't appear to contain words or patterns that may be obvious to an attacker.

这个密码似乎不包含攻击者可能一眼就能看出来的单词或规律。

1. `This password` 是主语。`doesn't appear` 是带时态的谓语动词组，展开为 `does not appear`。
2. `to contain …` 是 `appear` 后面的不定式结构。`words or patterns` 是 `contain` 的宾语。
3. `that may be obvious to an attacker` 修饰 `words or patterns`。把先行词补回去是 `The words or patterns may be obvious to an attacker.`，所以这里的 `that` 在定语从句里作主语，需要保留。
4. 在你之前那条提示的后半句 `you aren't reusing it for passwords saved across multiple services in Passwords` 中，`aren't reusing` 是现在进行时的否定形式。`saved …` 是过去分词短语，修饰 `passwords`，可以从 `passwords that are saved …` 来理解。

### 快速自测

1. `She sent me a link.` 和 `She made me nervous.` 中，`me` 和后面那部分各是什么成分？
2. `I think that the file is ready.` 和 `The file that contains the notes is ready.`，哪个 `that` 常可省？
3. `I'd already left when she called.` 和 `I'd call if I had her number.`，两个 `I'd` 分别怎么展开？
4. `I worked there for two years.` 和 `I've worked here for two years.` 都用了 `for`，时间视角有什么不同？
5. 把 `Where is the station?` 放到 `Could you tell me …?` 里，语序怎么改？

<details>
<summary>查看答案</summary>

1. `sent me a link` 中 `me` 是间接宾语，`a link` 是直接宾语。`made me nervous` 中 `me` 是宾语，`nervous` 是宾语补足语。
2. 第一处常可省，`the file is ready` 本身已有主语。第二处保留，`that` 是 `contains` 的主语。
3. 第一处 `I had`，说在她打电话前就离开了。第二处 `I would`，说如果有她的号码就会打电话。
4. `worked` 把这段经历放在过去。`have worked` 在这里可表示从过去开始并持续到现在。
5. `Could you tell me where the station is?` 内层用 `where the station is`。

</details>
